QR Radar — Test Bench

Live verification suite for real-time QR detection and HUD tracking

🚀 How to load and test in Firefox:

  1. Open a new tab in Firefox and navigate to about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
  2. Click "Load Temporary Add-on..." and select c:\Dev\qr_ext\manifest.json
  3. Return to this page, click the QR Radar icon in the Firefox toolbar (or press Alt + Q)
  4. Click "Start Real-Time Scanner" and choose this tab in the permission prompt
  5. Watch the glowing neon HUD frame and bounding box track all QR codes in real-time!
